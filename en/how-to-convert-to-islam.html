How to convert to Islam

To become a Muslim, say the Shahada and mean it. That declaration of faith is the conversion. You do not need a mosque, a ceremony, or witnesses.

After you convert

  1. Take a ritual bath (ghusl)
  2. Learn to pray (salah)
  3. A mosque can help you learn. It is not required to become Muslim.

Questions

How do I become a Muslim?

Say the Shahada with sincere belief. That is the conversion. You do not fill in a form or pay a fee.

Do I have to go to a mosque?

No. You can convert to Islam alone at home. A mosque is useful afterward for prayer and people to ask.

Do I have to say it in Arabic?

Arabic is the usual wording. If you understand the sentence and say it in English, you are Muslim. Practice the Arabic on this page.
